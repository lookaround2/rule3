## Authority Examples

- Graham v Graham, 2014 ABQB 615: relevance/materiality.
- Brookdale International v Crescent Point Energy, 2023 ABKB 120: objections, facts vs evidence/strategy, mixed remedies.
- CNOOC Petroleum North America ULC v 801 Seventh Inc, 2023 ABCA 97: corporate representative duty.
- H2 Canmore Apartments LP v Cormode & Dickson Construction Edmonton Ltd, 2024 ABKB 424: corporate representative preparation and undertaking marshalling.
- McDonald v Sproule Management GP Limited, 2023 ABKB 587: Rule 5.30 and undertaking-answer use.
- ShawCor, PetroFrontier, Goodswimmer, Nash, Hartson: privilege, waiver, and without-prejudice/settlement issues.

## Critical Distinctions

```text
Rule 5.25 determines whether a question must be answered or objection upheld.
Rule 5.30 supplies the undertaking mechanism when the proper answer/record is not immediately available.
Rule 5.4 modifies analysis for corporate representatives.
Part 6 questioning undertakings are analogue-only and more restricted.
Rule 5.33 implied undertaking is separate from Rule 5.30 discovery undertakings.
```
