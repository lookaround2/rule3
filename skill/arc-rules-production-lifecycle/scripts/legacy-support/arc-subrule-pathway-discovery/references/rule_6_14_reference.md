## Case Ladder Examples

- Bahcheli v Yorkton Securities Inc, 2012 ABCA 166: correctness/non-deferential review; Rule 6.14(3) on-record language is about evidence, not deference.
- Gudzinski Estate v Allianz Global Risks US Insurance Co, 2012 ABCA 5: new evidence changes fact-assessment posture; no sense reviewing facts based on incomplete record.
- Challis v Maverick Oilfield Services Ltd, 2023 ABKB 514: de novo-like elements but still appeal on record and notice compliance.
- Bank of Montreal v Luciano, 2024 ABKB 314: correctness/de novo language and self-represented compliance expectations.

## Safe Formulation

A Rule 6.14 appeal is best described as an appeal on the record before the Applications Judge plus relevant and material additional evidence the judge permits. It is non-deferential/correctness under Bahcheli unless current binding law changes that posture, but it should not be overcalled as an unrestricted fresh hearing.
