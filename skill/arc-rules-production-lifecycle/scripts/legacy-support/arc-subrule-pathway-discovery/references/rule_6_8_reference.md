## Case Ladder Examples

- Feeney v Alberta, 2021 ABCA 255: threshold for court-ordered questioning; need sense of questions and relevance.
- Guillevin International Co v Barry, 2022 ABCA 144: prima facie right / set-aside / abuse and directions pathway.
- Dechant v Law Society of Alberta, 2000 ABCA 265: predecessor-rule root authority; evidence becomes part of applicant's case; no cherry-picking.
- Gow Estate (Re), 2021 ABQB 305: not discovery/fishing; relevant/material to motion; records precision; gap-filling.
- Great North Equipment Inc v Penney, 2024 ABKB 391: non-party Rule 6.8 undertaking limits; no Part 5 undertaking importation by default.
- Kudzin v APM Construction Services Inc, 2023 ABKB 425: Rule 6.11 and Part 5 transcript-use interaction.

## Core Distinctions

```text
Rule 6.8 is application-evidence questioning, not ordinary discovery.
Transcript may become application evidence; the examiner cannot necessarily cherry-pick.
Rule 5.30 undertakings do not automatically apply to non-party Rule 6.8 witnesses.
Rule 6.11 remains a separate application-evidence gate.
```
